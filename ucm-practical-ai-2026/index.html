I stopped focusing on the how.
Now I focus on the what.

Design instead of code. Unit tests that actually exist (I don't have to write them). Less time on infrastructure. Try new things and iterate faster than I could build normally.

What I've learned the hard way

  1. It ignores instructions
  2. It goes down rabbit holes
  3. It guesses when you're vague
  4. It's confidently wrong
  5. It's only as good as its context
  6. It tells you what you want to hear

Do you use AI outside of coding?

Quick answers instead of Google. Research and learning. Business questions. Presentations (like this one). Trip planning. Home tech support. Product design and CAD: the Fusion 360 story.

    Find a mentorGo to user groups and meetupsAttend conferences
